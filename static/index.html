How detection works
  • Lying = torso angle > 60° from vertical AND (box wider than 1.2× height OR body spread horizontal).
  • Fall = person was upright less than 1.5 s before lying, or hips dropped ≥ 40% of body height.
  • Fall + no movement for 10 s → emergency alert.
  • On the floor 30 s (even if moving) → alert ("can't get up").
  • Still down → reminder every 2 min. Stand up for 3 s → reset.
  • Sitting close to the camera is not a fall (no torso tilt).
